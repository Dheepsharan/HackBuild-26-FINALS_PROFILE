🛡️ Server-Enforced Workflow

Every order transition is validated by Cloud Functions and Admin SDK, never client-side code.

⏱️ Work-Hour-Aware Queue

Ready estimates dynamically compute based on existing backlog, worker capacity, and shop opening hours.

⚡ Dual Demo & Live Modes

Zero-friction demo mode for evaluation, switches to live Firebase production with a single config key.

Comparing directly against traditional offline chaos and complex e-commerce platforms.

Technical Approach

Robust, scalable, and secure architecture built on Google Cloud & Firebase.

🎙️ Pitch Teleprompter & Cues

0:00 - 0:20 (20s)
Dheep Sharan · Intro

"Good morning judges! We are team igniora, and today we're proud to present CraftPulse for the Open Innovation track at Hack Build'26. CraftPulse is a hyperlocal platform designed to discover, order, and track custom services from your neighbourhood artisans—from tailors and cobblers to bakers and woodworkers."

0:20 - 0:50 (30s)
Sankara Magesh · The Problem

"Right now, millions of skilled local makers remain completely invisible online. When Priya's shoe splits on her way to work, she has to physically visit three shops, hears three arbitrary verbal prices, and leaves with zero ready-time estimate and no receipt. Customers face price uncertainty, while artisans struggle to organize customer orders."

0:50 - 1:15 (25s)
Dheeranjai · The Solution

"CraftPulse bridges this gap with a 5-step collaborative workflow: Discovery by distance and ratings, instant digital work requests, fixed or custom quotation negotiation, real-time queue tracking, and pickup confirmation with itemized invoices."

1:15 - 1:35 (20s)
Team · Uniqueness

"Unlike generic e-commerce marketplaces or chaotic WhatsApp chats, CraftPulse calculates dynamic wait times using a work-hour-aware queue engine. Most importantly, all state transitions are strictly validated server-side by Cloud Functions."

1:35 - 1:55 (20s)
Team · Tech Stack

"Under the hood, CraftPulse uses a responsive vanilla JS frontend coupled with Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore for real-time reads, and Cloud Functions running an Admin SDK finite-state machine. Our Firestore security rules completely deny direct client writes, ensuring tamper-proof workflow integrity."

1:55 - 2:35 (40s)
Live Prototype Walkthrough

"Here is our live prototype running right now. As a customer, you can browse nearby ateliers, filter by craft, and place a custom service request. Now, switching to the Artisan Maker Studio, the order immediately arrives in the workbench queue. The artisan accepts it, moves it to 'In Progress', and when complete, marks it 'Ready'. The customer receives a notification, scans to pay via UPI, and downloads a clean, printable invoice."

2:35 - 2:50 (15s)
Impact & Roadmap

"CraftPulse digitizes micro-economies at zero friction. Our roadmap includes Cloud Storage portfolio uploads, maker KYC identity verification, direct UPI settlement escrows, and a React Native mobile application."

2:50 - 3:00 (10s)
Closing

"Thank you for your time! We are Team igniora, building what's next with CraftPulse. We'd love to answer any questions!"

Step 05 Selecting a Service & Garment Measurements
🎙️ What to Say:
"When a patron selects a service — say, custom tailoring from Asha's Silk Atelier or sneaker restoration from Footshop — CraftPulse removes all ambiguity. For tailoring, our system autofills saved body measurements — chest, waist, shoulder, and length — directly from the user profile, eliminating the need to visit the shop with a tape measure. Patrons also choose their fulfillment: shop pickup or doorstep courier delivery."
🖥️ On Screen:
Click on Footshop or Asha's Atelier ➔ Click Order Piece ➔ Show the pre-filled service name, price, and measurement inputs.

Step 12 Invoice Generation & UPI Payment Method
🎙️ What to Say:
"Finally, settling the order is frictionless. Customers can choose Cash on Delivery or tap Online Payment.
When they choose online payment, CraftPulse opens a Scan & Pay UPI Modal with a dedicated QR code compatible with Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
Once simulated, the system automatically generates an official, printable Tax Invoice complete with GST breakdown, atelier license details, and Porter delivery charges."
🖥️ On Screen:
  • Click Pay & Generate Invoice on the customer card.
  • Show the UPI QR Code modal with Google Pay / PhonePe badges.
  • Click Simulate Successful Payment ➔ The official printable Tax Invoice pops up immediately on screen.

🛡️ Quick Reference: Anticipated Judge Questions & Winning Answers

Anticipated Question Winning Answer Key Points
What is your revenue model? 1. ₹10–₹15 convenience markup on Porter courier dispatches.
2. Guild Pro subscription (₹499/mo) for queue analytics, SMS alerts & automated bookkeeping.
3. 1.5% micro-fee on escrow UPI payments.
Why not just use WhatsApp Business? Artisans lose hours answering "Is it ready?" and tracking misplaced physical slips. CraftPulse automates live queue status, offers doorstep courier booking, and issues legal GST tax invoices.
How does offline/low connectivity work? Built as an offline-first PWA using localStorage caching and background sync when connection restores.
Is this actually deployed? Yes, live on production with edge CDN and HTTPS at https://craftpulse-fxly.netlify.app.
